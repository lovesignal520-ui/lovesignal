# LoveSignal monetization order

## 1. Build approval-ready content first

Publish the included About, Privacy, Terms, DMCA, Affiliate Disclosure and original guide pages. Add more original guides and show descriptions regularly. Repair broken images and episode-count mismatches before applying to an ad network.

## 2. Keep Ko-fi as the first low-risk income option

The app already includes a support button. Explain what donations support, such as hosting, research and show-link maintenance. Never promise access to copyrighted episodes in exchange for a donation.

## 3. Add relevant affiliate links

Apply only to official, licensed streaming or entertainment services that allow your traffic source and country. Put the disclosure beside each link and use `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"`. Do not disguise an affiliate link as an episode button.

## 4. Apply for AdSense after the site is substantial

Wait until the site has consistent original text, clear navigation, working legal pages and enough useful content for visitors. After approval, replace the placeholder in `MONETIZATION-SNIPPET.html`. Never click your own ads, ask visitors to click, or place ads where they may be mistaken for navigation or episode controls.

## 5. Add sponsorship packages after traffic grows

Possible products: a clearly labeled homepage sponsor card, newsletter mention, or sponsored editorial guide. Keep sponsored placements separate from rankings and label them prominently.

## Recommended page placement

- Home: one responsive ad after useful content, not above the hero.
- Guide pages: one ad after the introduction and one near the end only when article length supports it.
- Show details: avoid putting an ad immediately beside episode buttons.
- Mobile: do not let ads cover the fixed bottom navigation.

## Metrics to watch

Track organic visits, search queries, outbound clicks to official platforms, watchlist saves, broken-link reports and returning visitors. Do not optimize only for page views; useful repeat visits are more valuable.
