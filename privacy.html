Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This policy explains what LoveSignal stores and how analytics, forms and external links may process information.

Information stored on your device

LoveSignal uses browser local storage to save your watchlist, reminder choices and optional profile name, avatar and bio. This information normally remains in your browser unless you clear site data.

Analytics

LoveSignal uses Google Analytics to understand general site usage. Google may receive technical information such as device/browser details, approximate location derived from IP address, pages viewed and interaction events. The site owner should configure consent controls where required by applicable law.

Feedback and show-request forms

When the site owner connects a form provider, information submitted through a form is sent to that provider and to the site owner. Do not submit passwords, payment-card information or other sensitive data.

External links

Links to video platforms, donation services and other websites are governed by those services’ own privacy policies.

Advertising and affiliate links

If advertising or affiliate links are activated, third parties may use cookies or similar technologies as described in their own policies. LoveSignal will label advertisements and disclose compensated links.

Your choices

  • Clear local data through your browser settings.
  • Use browser privacy or tracking controls.
  • Do not submit optional profile or form information.

Contact

Use the Feedback option in the LoveSignal app for privacy questions. Add a monitored contact email here before applying for ad programs.
