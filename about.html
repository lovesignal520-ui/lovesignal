About LoveSignal

A simpler way to discover Asian dating reality shows

LoveSignal helps viewers browse shows, save a personal watchlist, read concise descriptions, and open official or authorized viewing links.

What LoveSignal does

  • Organizes shows by country, format, release status and themes.
  • Saves a watchlist and profile preferences locally on the visitor’s device.
  • Links to trailers and episodes hosted by official or authorized publishers.
  • Publishes original guides that help new viewers understand the genre.
Important: LoveSignal does not host, upload or stream video files. Titles, logos, artwork and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.

Editorial approach

Every show page should be checked for a clear title, accurate release status, a useful original description, matching episode counts and working source links. We correct mistakes when they are reported.

Contact and corrections

Use the Feedback or Request a Show form inside the app. The site owner should connect those forms to a receiving service before publication.
