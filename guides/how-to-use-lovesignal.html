How to use LoveSignal

Updated August 5, 2026

LoveSignal is designed as a lightweight show-discovery app. Your watchlist, reminders and optional profile details are saved in your browser.

Search and browse

Use the search box for a title, country, category or theme. The Shows tab separates recent, completed, current-year releases and confirmed upcoming titles.

Save a watchlist

Open a show and select “Add to My List.” The choice is stored on the current device and browser. It will not automatically sync to another phone.

Open viewing links

Episode and trailer buttons open external sites in a new tab. Verify the destination is an official or authorized source before watching.

Share a show

The Share button creates a direct URL containing the show identifier. When someone opens that URL, LoveSignal attempts to open the matching detail screen.

Privacy and clearing data

Use your browser’s site-data controls to remove the locally stored watchlist, reminders and profile. Read the Privacy Policy for analytics and form-provider details.
